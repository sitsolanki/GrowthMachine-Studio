What affects the cost of a business website?

Pages, content and design detail

A single-purpose landing page is usually a smaller project than a multi-page site with separate service, about, portfolio and contact pages. Original photography, detailed copy, custom illustrations and repeated design revisions also take planning and production time. Bringing your logo, service details, images and preferred examples to the first discussion helps define the work.

Features and integrations

WhatsApp and phone links are simpler than online payments, booking calendars, account logins, inventory systems or integrations with other software. List the actions visitors need to take, such as requesting an appointment or asking about a product. A clear requirement helps a designer recommend the simplest useful implementation instead of adding features you may not need.

Domain, hosting and future changes

Ask who will own the domain, hosting account and website files, and whether recurring costs apply. GrowthMachine Studio's approach is for the client to buy the domain and any paid services in their own name; setup guidance is available. Also agree who can update prices, contact details and images after launch, and whether ongoing maintenance is part of the quote.

How to choose the right project scope

  • Choose a landing page when you have one main service or campaign and want visitors to call, message or submit an enquiry.
  • Choose a business website when customers need to understand several services, check your credentials and find reliable contact details.
  • Choose a product catalogue when visitors need to browse product categories before contacting you about an order.

For any option, prioritise fast mobile use, readable service information, useful contact details and a clear next step. A website can support your marketing, but it does not guarantee enquiries or a particular position in Google results.

Questions to ask a web designer before paying

  • What pages, features, content and revisions are included in the written scope?
  • Who owns the domain, hosting account, source files and third-party accounts?
  • What recurring charges should I budget for after launch?
  • Will the site work on mobile, and who is responsible for testing the contact form and links?
  • What search setup is included, and what ongoing SEO work is outside the project?
  • How will I request updates or get help after the site is live?

Search setup can include accurate page titles and descriptions, crawlable page content, mobile-friendly design and a sitemap. Those are technical foundations, not a ranking guarantee. Google rankings also depend on competition, how useful and trustworthy your content is, local visibility and time. Keep your business name, address and phone number consistent wherever your business is listed, and only claim information you can verify.
