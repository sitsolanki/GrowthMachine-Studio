Business Website Design Cost in Delhi | 2026 Pricing Guide

Planning a business website in Delhi? This 2026 guide breaks down typical package pricing, what influences the final cost, and how to choose the right scope for your goals.

Why a Business Website Matters

A professional business website helps customers understand your services, trust your brand, and contact you quickly. For Delhi businesses competing in local and online markets, a clear, mobile-friendly website acts as your digital storefront—available 24/7 to answer questions and generate enquiries.

Beyond credibility, the right website structure supports lead generation, showcases your offerings, and gives you a platform you control. Whether you need a focused landing page or a multi-page site, investing in the right scope can save time and improve results compared to relying only on social profiles or directories.

What Affects the Cost of a Business Website?

Pages, content, and design complexity

A single landing page typically costs less than a website with multiple service pages, galleries, or detailed content sections. Custom layouts, branded visuals, copywriting support, and revision rounds can also influence the overall effort and price.

Features and integrations

Basic contact forms, call buttons, and WhatsApp links are straightforward. Costs can increase with features like booking systems, payment integrations, advanced forms, multilingual content, or connections to external tools and CRMs.

Domain, hosting, and ongoing maintenance

Clarify who purchases and owns the domain, hosting, and any paid services. Ongoing costs—such as renewals, content updates, backups, or performance monitoring—may be separate from the initial build. Agree on post-launch support and update responsibilities before starting.

How to Choose the Right Scope

  • Choose Starter if you need a fast, focused page to promote one service or collect enquiries from ads and local searches.
  • Choose Business if you need a professional presence with room to explain multiple services, build trust, and guide visitors to contact you.
  • Choose Catalog if your priority is showcasing products or categories so customers can browse and enquire easily.

Start with the simplest scope that meets your immediate goal. You can expand later as your business grows, but a clear initial plan helps control cost and launch faster.

Questions to Ask Before Hiring a Web Designer

  • What pages, sections, features, and revisions are included in the quoted price?
  • Who will own the domain, hosting account, website files, and third-party tools?
  • Are there any recurring costs after launch, and what do they cover?
  • Will the site be mobile-responsive, fast-loading, and tested across devices and browsers?
  • What basic SEO setup is included (titles, meta descriptions, sitemap), and what is excluded?
  • How are content updates, bug fixes, and support handled after the website goes live?

Clear answers to these questions help you compare quotes fairly and avoid surprises. Remember, a website can improve your online presence and enquiry flow, but no designer can guarantee specific rankings or sales results.
